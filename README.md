# Art4Everyone

Kyungjin Bae 배경진 (team lead), with a team of 4. Mobile web app, 2026

A museum companion guide for ages 50–64. Enter the time you have, and it recommends as many works as that time allows, guides you to the next one with AR, and shows the nearest rest area before you tire.

<img src="docs/images/art4everyone-start.jpg" alt="Art4Everyone start screen" width="320">

**[Try the web app](https://art4everyone-repo.vercel.app)**, **[Read the case study](https://kellyb1024.github.io/en/art4everyone.html)**

## Problem

- People aged 50–64 are assumed to visit museums less because they struggle with digital tools, yet all 31 surveyed used smartphones with ease
- The real barriers were fatigue from standing and small label text. Existing museum apps keep eyes on the screen for the whole visit
- No way to see less but see well, at one's own stamina and pace

## Process

- Visits to three senior centers, a survey of 31 people aged 50–64, 4 focus groups (15 people) and observation at the National Museum of Korea
- Less time on the phone, instead of more app features. Large type and one action per screen to shorten the flow
- Refined through remote testing with 6 people

## Result

- A web app that plans each visit around the visitor's time and energy, widening cultural access in an aging society
- Selected for the Yonsei IHEI Social Innovation Town workstation

## My role
